RA-One

Train with your Pack.

RA-One is a training app for small groups. You post your workouts to a private circle you choose, take on challenges together, and keep each other honest. No followers, no feed of strangers, no algorithm.

Support

Need help, found a bug, or want to delete your account?

Email ra.one.bcn@gmail.com and you'll get a reply from a person, usually within a couple of days.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?
Settings → Delete account, inside the app. That removes your profile, posts, messages, check-ins and uploaded photos. If you can't get into the app, email us and we'll do it for you.

Who can see my posts?
Only people in your Pack — and members of a challenge, if you tag the post to one. Nothing is public. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

How do I join someone's Pack?
Both people have to agree. One sends an invite, the other accepts. Either of you can leave at any time.

I forgot my password.
Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen. If the email doesn't arrive, check spam, then email us.

Privacy

RA-One has no advertising, no trackers, and no third-party analytics, and your data is never sold or shared with data brokers. Read the privacy policy.
