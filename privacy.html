RA-One Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

RA-One is a training app for small groups. You post workouts to a private circle you choose — your Pack — and take part in shared challenges. This policy describes exactly what the app stores, why, who can see it, and how to get rid of it.

The service is operated by Diego Riviere Padilha (New York, United States), the data controller for the purposes of Brazil's Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD) and equivalent laws.

Account information

Social activity

  • Pack membership and invitations — who you have invited, who has invited you, and who is in your Pack.
  • Challenges — which challenges you have joined, your daily check-ins, and any notes you attach to them.
  • Reactions and comments on posts.
  • Stories — photos or videos that are automatically deleted 24 hours after posting.
  • Direct messages — the text you send, any attachments, and reactions. Messages are only possible between people who are in each other's Pack.

Location

RA-One does not track your location in the background and does not attach location to posts.

The app offers one optional feature that uses location: live location sharing inside a direct message. If you start it, your approximate coordinates are shared with that one conversation, for a duration you set, and the record is automatically deleted when it expires. You can stop it at any time. If you never start it, no location data is stored. See section 3.

Device and technical data

  • Push notification token, if you allow notifications — so we can deliver them. Removing notification permission or deleting the app invalidates it.
  • Server logs kept by our infrastructure providers, which include IP address and timestamps, retained for a short period for security and debugging.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers. No advertising, and no ad networks.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No contacts, calendar, or microphone access.
  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with data brokers. Ever.

3. Sensitive data

Two things RA-One stores need to be called out specifically, because Brazilian law (LGPD, Art. 5, II) treats them as sensitive personal data requiring your specific, separate consent:

  • Heart rate. If you include average heart rate on a post, that is health data.
  • Location. Live location sharing reveals where you are.

Both are entirely optional and off unless you turn them on. The app asks for each separately, at the moment you first use it, and explains what it is for. You can withdraw consent at any time: stop including heart rate, stop sharing live location, and delete any past posts that contain them.

We do not use this data for anything other than displaying it to the people you shared it with. It is never used for profiling, advertising, or automated decisions about you, and it is never shared with insurers, employers, or any third party.

5. How long we keep it

  • Stories — deleted automatically 24 hours after posting.
  • Live location — deleted automatically when the sharing period you set expires.
  • Posts, messages, check-ins, profile — kept until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Server logs — kept briefly by our providers for security and debugging.

6. Your rights

Under the LGPD, and under equivalent rights in other jurisdictions, you can:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you, and get a copy in a portable format.
  • Correct anything inaccurate — most of it you can edit in the app directly.
  • Delete your account and its data.
  • Withdraw consent for heart rate or location at any time, without losing access to the rest of the app.
  • Object to a particular use, or ask us to restrict it.
  • Complain to Brazil's data protection authority, the ANPD, or to your local regulator.

To exercise any of these, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. We will respond within 15 days, the period the LGPD sets.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from within the app, under Settings. When you do, we permanently delete your profile, posts, messages, check-ins, Pack memberships, and the photos and videos you uploaded. Content you sent to other people's conversations may remain visible to them as part of their own message history, shown without your name attached.

7. Children

RA-One is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Access to every piece of data described here is enforced by row-level security rules on the database itself, not only by the app. That means a bug in the app cannot expose data the server would refuse to hand over. Traffic is encrypted in transit. Passwords are hashed and never visible to us.

No system is perfect. If we ever discover a breach affecting your data, we will tell you and notify the ANPD, as the law requires.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that affects how your data is used, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
